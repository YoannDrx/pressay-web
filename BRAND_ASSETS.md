# Brand assets used by the compatibility marquee

The application logos rendered by `components/app-logo-marquee.tsx` are bundled
at build time from `@iconify-json/logos` and `@iconify-json/simple-icons`. They are not hotlinked and no request
is made to a third-party icon CDN.

Iconify's Logos collection aggregates official or community-maintained brand
artwork and ships collection metadata under the MIT licence. Individual product
names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners. Their presence
indicates compatibility only; it does not imply sponsorship or partnership.

Cursor uses the Simple Icons asset distributed under CC0 1.0; the former
`precursor` glyph was intentionally removed because it represented a different
product.

Apple's mark is used as a platform indicator for Mail and Notes because Apple
does not publish redistributable standalone SVG application marks for those two
apps. The visible labels remain explicit, and no modified Apple app icon is
presented as official artwork.
